# Field Notes

Published architecture write-ups about this repository: what it does, where
its boundaries sit, and what its results do and do not establish. Unlike
[RFCs](../rfcs/), which propose work before it exists, and
[ADRs](../adrs/), which record a single decision, a field note reviews
shipped work after the fact for an outside reader.

Field notes are published under [Field Notes](https://arboreng.com/field-notes)
on the Arbor Engineering Group site rather than duplicated here, so there is
one canonical copy to read and cite.

- **Architecture Field Note: quantum-foundry** (August 2026) — the repository
  as a whole: the maturity ladder and why nothing sits at `reference`, the
  `Oracle`/`Executor` seams, what factoring 15 costs at three distances from
  real hardware, the Grover iteration-count bug, and what the benchmarks
  decline to flatter.
